Particles

This environment is based on the main streets of my hometown, Tepatitlán de Morelos. Many of the facades are from real houses in Tepatitlán. This class project represents an incredible challenge for me — unlike my classmates, I built the entire environment from scratch, from the props to the houses. To finish, I added water and fire to the street lamps. The main challenge was creating the particle effects.
