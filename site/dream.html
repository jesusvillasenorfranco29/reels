Dream

This environment is born from a dream layered with another. The idea is simple: owning a CRF 250, being at the beach — a quiet, peaceful place — with a cold drink in hand. The feeling of having a weekend like that, soaking up the sun, the water, playing with a ball, or just lying down listening to the music I grew up with. Or maybe just riding that motorcycle along the shore. It's something I love, and it's what inspired me to bring this scene to life.
